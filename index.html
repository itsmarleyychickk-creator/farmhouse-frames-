OUR STORY

We photograph what feels like home.

Farmhouse Frames is built around a simple idea: local places can become some of the most meaningful art we own.

We capture familiar landscapes, landmarks, farms, streets, seasons and small hometown details, then turn those photographs into wall art, décor and gifts made for homes near and far.

GOOD TO KNOW

Made-to-order details.

Who makes and ships my order?

Farmhouse Frames creates the product selection and photography. Finished pieces are professionally produced by our printing partner and shipped directly to the customer.

Are the products kept in stock?

Most pieces are made to order rather than stored as finished inventory.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact Farmhouse Frames with your order details and photos of the issue. We’ll document the problem and work through the production-partner resolution process.

Can I request a different local location?

Yes. Custom-location requests can be submitted below. Availability depends on whether we already have suitable photography or can reasonably capture the requested location.
